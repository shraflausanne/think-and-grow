# Think and Grow

A personal goals app built on the main frameworks of Napoleon Hill's *Think and Grow Rich* (1937): the six steps to a definite aim, a daily reading ritual with a streak, a self-rating of the 13 principles, a Master Mind list, the six fears and the four habits of persistence.

- Runs entirely in the browser. No account, no server.
- Your entries are saved on your phone only (browser storage). They do not sync to other devices.
- To change any wording, edit the block marked `EDITABLE CONTENT` near the top of the script in `index.html`.
